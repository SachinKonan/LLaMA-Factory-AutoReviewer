arXiv label and leakage filters
Template and venue recognition Detect conference style files and map papers to venue-year cells with ground-truth paperlists.
Back-reference paperlists Match title/authors to proceedings lists; accepts require a main-conference paperlist match.
Reject construction Use implied main-conference rejects only where paperlist coverage exists; drop no-ground-truth cells.
Validity filters Remove workshop, student, tutorial, short, de-anonymized, and venue-mismatched artifacts.
Distribution controls Require body pages ≥ 6 and cap reject length at the venue accept p95 before balancing.
Common PDF-to-data pipeline

PDF artifact

  • OpenReview PDFs and compiled arXiv PDFs enter the same extraction path.
  • All downstream examples are built from paper content, not review text.

MinerU extraction

  • Run MinerU to produce markdown and structured content lists.
  • Locate references, appendix, and body boundary; discard broken extractions.

Content filtering

  • Strip references and leakage-prone tokens from text.
  • Clip page images to the paper body and verify image/text integrity.
